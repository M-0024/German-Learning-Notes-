## 7. Quick-Reference Tips

- Always learn a noun **with its article** (der/die/das) — never in isolation.
- Read each example sentence aloud, not just the word — the sentence is what actually builds recall.
- A1 vocabulary is the "survival kit" — prioritize fluency of recall over breadth.
- Move to German-A2-Vocabulary-Complete.md once these feel automatic, not just recognizable.
